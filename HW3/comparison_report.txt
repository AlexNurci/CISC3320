Priority convention:
Lower priority number = higher priority.
Priority ties use earlier arrival, then input order.

MLFQ rules:
Queues: Q0 and Q1
Q0 quantum: 4
Q1 quantum: 8
New processes enter Q0.
Using a full Q0 quantum causes demotion to Q1.
Using a full Q1 quantum causes requeueing in Q1.
Same-queue order is FIFO.
Aging: none.
